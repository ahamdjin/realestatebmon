    Disclosure built in

    AB 723-ready, so the tour helps the listing instead of risking it.

    Since January 1, 2026, California requires listing images that are digitally altered beyond simple fixes to be disclosed, with the unaltered originals available. AI tours change images by definition, so we treat disclosure as part of the product.

    • Visible labeling on the tour page, the film and every social cut.
    • A link or QR code to your original photos, included with each tour.
    • A check for invented features, such as extra windows, rooms or views, before you see a draft.

    This is how we build our product, not legal advice. Confirm your obligations with your broker.
